Strategic finding

Tiruppur's problem is not a lack of attractions. The official district material already presents temples, dams and forest/wildlife as tourism categories. The opportunity is to package them into routes, experiences and trip decisions.

The strongest differentiation is the combination of industrial identity + Western Ghats access + Kongu heritage.

Seasonality signal

The district's Panchalinga Falls page specifically identifies November to June as a favourable period to enjoy the falls fully. This suggests the planner should eventually include seasonal scoring rather than treating every attraction as year-round.

Weather and attraction operating conditions should be checked live before a production launch.
